### Taskbar
- **The taskbar no longer disappears a few minutes after logging in.** The top bar and the taskbar are two copies of the same bar program (waybar), which registers itself as a single app on the desktop's message bus; the second copy joined the first instead of running on its own and was shut down by an internal error about five minutes later. The taskbar now runs on its own private message bus, so the two never clash. Apps opened from the taskbar (Start menu, Lumen menu, restoring windows) work as before.
- **The bar watchdog from v1.0.7 is fixed**: it didn't recognise a bar it had just restarted and kept starting more; and the taskbar's restarts could hold back the top bar's. Each bar is now watched separately and comes back within about 10 seconds if it ever stops.

### Everything from v1.0.7
- Installs with no internet at all, entirely from the USB stick.
- Alongside Windows: small free space is handled (30 GB minimum) instead of refused.
- Chromium, Brave and Google Chrome run as native Wayland apps.

### Testing
- 19 virtual machines, including new checks that the taskbar is still running at the end of every test and that the top bar comes back after being killed.

Installed v1.0.7? Run `lumen update` (Lumen menu → Update) to get the taskbar fix without reinstalling.
